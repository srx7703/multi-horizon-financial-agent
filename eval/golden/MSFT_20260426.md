## Executive summary

Microsoft's Q2 FY26 10-Q (period ending Dec 31 2025) shows revenue of **$81.27 billion, up 16.7% YoY** [10-Q] and net income of **$38.46 billion (+59.5% YoY)** [10-Q]. The headline net-income jump is **inflated by a $9.93 billion non-recurring gain on investments and derivatives** — strip it out and the underlying **operating income of $38.28 billion grew a more moderate 20.9% YoY** [10-Q]. Diluted EPS came in at **$5.16 vs $3.23** in the prior-year period (+59.8%) [10-Q].

The single most material disclosure is **capital expenditures: $29.88 billion in the quarter, up 89.0% YoY** [10-Q]. Total assets grew to **$665.30B from $619.00B** at FY25 close, while cash + short-term investments **fell from $94.57B to $89.46B** — capital is being actively redeployed into AI / cloud infrastructure rather than parked [10-Q]. Management tone is "bullish"; the analyst-note flags the same trade-off — blowout headline, but the durability story is whether the CapEx ROI shows up.

The stock has **fallen 9.71% over the trailing 3 months** (close $470.28 → $424.62 from 2026-01-26 to 2026-04-24) [market]. The 3-month intraday range $356.28 – $483.74 [market] — and notably the 3-month low of **$356.28 is also the 52-week low** [market], meaning MSFT made its annual trough during this window. Trailing P/E **26.6x**, forward P/E **22.4x**, market cap ≈ **$3.16 trillion** [market-info].
