## Key risks

The Q3 FY25 10-Q's structured `new_risks` field surfaces **measurement risks** rather than financial risks (consistent with the management tone "cautious"):

1. **Loss of access to data signals** due to third-party browser or mobile platform actions [10-Q]
2. **Regulatory / legislative actions** impacting the stability or accuracy of reported "Family" metrics (DAP, ARPP) [10-Q]
3. **Limited visibility into encrypted products** (e.g. WhatsApp) — affecting the ability to match user accounts and identify violating accounts (estimated **<3% of worldwide DAP**) [10-Q]

Beyond these textual risks, the **CapEx-financed asset buildup** (P&E +$39B; cash −$33.7B) is itself a forward risk if AI infrastructure ROI undershoots [10-Q].
